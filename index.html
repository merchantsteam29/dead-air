    How to play

    You're a tiny ghost-hunting channel. Every night the van drops you somewhere abandoned. Find the weird stuff and film it. Every second of good footage is views. Three nights a season; hit the season's view quota or the channel gets terminated.

    • Grab the camcorder at the van. Hold Right click to film. Weird things in frame, close up and lit, are worth the most. Filming the same thing for ages gets boring for viewers.
    • Bring the tape back. When everyone alive is at the van, hold E to drive away. At 6 a.m. the van leaves anyway. Tapes left out there are lost, and so is anyone outside.
    • Mr. Applause hears voices and running. Keep it down. Or don't: if he gets your friend on camera, that's the best footage of the night. A thrown object stuns him.
    • The hand steals whatever you're holding. Throw something at it.
    • Dead? Your friends can carry (or throw) your body back to the van. A few seconds there and you're back.
    • Donations pop up: viewers pay you to film something. First camcorder to do it gets the bonus.
    • The van's kit (free every night): two walkie-talkies (talk across the map; monsters hear the static), an EMF meter (beeps near weird stuff), a flash camera (3 shots, stuns what's in front), a spare battery (click next to a camcorder).
    • Roles: Camera op (battery lasts longer), Host (talking on camera pays 3x), Runner (more stamina, faster), Tech (a built-in EMF meter, sees the Critic up close). The van gets fancier as your channel grows, with a small perk at each level.
    • Places: lots, malls, hospitals and schools, each with something weird of its own. Night events: fog, storms, power cuts, blood moons (double views) and blood rain.
    • Blood rain drains your stamina and battery outside. Get under a roof, or film it for double views.
    • Your friends' screams, falls and arguments on camera count too, and the best 8 seconds of every tape go in the upload, with sound.
